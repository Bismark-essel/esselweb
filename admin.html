Photographs

Drop pictures in. Each one is resized to four widths and converted to WebP automatically — you never have to think about file sizes or names. The original is kept untouched in originals/.

Drop photographs here or click to choose · JPG, PNG or WebP · 2000 px wide
Export 2000 px on the WIDTH — portraits 2000 × 2500 (4:5), landscapes 2000 × 1333 (3:2). 2000 is the largest version this editor builds, so a 6000 px file is thrown away after a long wait. Under 1200 px wide it warns you, because it will look soft on a laptop.
Send a high-quality JPEG in sRGB and do not compress it first — the WebP is made here at 90 / 88 / 84 / 80 depending on size. Compressing twice is what makes skin look plastic.
Crop to the ratio before exporting. Each photograph keeps the shape you give it, so one 3:2 frame dropped into a 4:5 collection leaves a step in the grid.

Collections

Add, rename, reorder or remove. The website follows — the menu, the collections page and every grid are built from this list. Renaming is safe. Removing a collection that still has photographs is refused, so work cannot be lost by accident.

The opening screen

One photograph fills the first screen. Keep the middle of the frame quiet and darker, put the subject slightly to one side, and leave the top clear — the name sits across the centre.

Film

Films are shown as a single still that opens YouTube when clicked. No player is embedded, so the page stays as light as it is now and nothing on it depends on YouTube still working the same way in three years.

Choose the still the way you would choose a photograph — it sits beside his stills and is judged the same.
